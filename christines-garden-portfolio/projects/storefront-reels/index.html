This is just a sample of the work: four projects out of several I’ve made for Bethesda Green’s small business community.

Home & Organizing

Carrie It Away

Decluttering & organizing, one home at a time

Carrie helps clients transform crowded spaces using her own tips and tricks. Her home is also her business headquarters, though, so there wasn’t a tidy studio to film in. We shot it anyway, setting up in a crowded library and, at one point, her car, to catch her in the middle of the real work.

A national advisory firm, made local

CohnReznick is a major accounting, tax, and advisory firm helping organizations manage risk and grow. The brief called for a polished, corporate feel, while still making room to highlight the firm’s sustainability commitments on camera.

Legal

Nazareth Bonifacino

A woman-owned law firm, on their own terms

Nazareth Bonifacino puts joyful purpose and thoughtful guidance at the center of its practice. The video was built to show the people behind the firm, in a way that still feels as warm as the welcome you’d get walking through their door.

Community Nonprofit

Prathertown CDC

Preserving history, building what’s next

Prathertown Community Development Corporation works to preserve the neighborhood’s history while advancing housing, workforce development, education, and financial literacy. This piece honors that past while pointing toward the legacy the community is still building.

All four pieces were shot on location with each business’s own team, in the middle of a real working day, then edited for vertical, social-first viewing. Clips above are compressed for a quick preview, so full-resolution files are available on request.
